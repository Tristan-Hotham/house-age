How old is your neighbourhood?

Every small area (LSOA) of England and Wales, coloured by when its homes were built, from the Council Tax lists of 2025. Choose what to colour by at the bottom; tap an area for its full age profile. England and Wales only.

    Sources and licences
    • Homes by build period, Council Tax band and property type: Valuation Office Agency (2025), Council Tax: stock of properties, 2025, tables CTSOP4.1 and CTSOP3.1, data at 31 March 2025. Official Statistics, Open Government Licence v3.0. Counts are rounded to the nearest 10 and counts under five are suppressed by the VOA.
    • Small-area boundaries: Office for National Statistics, Lower layer Super Output Areas (December 2021) boundaries, generalised clipped, Open Government Licence v3.0 (contains OS data © Crown copyright and database right), via the GitHub copy in drkane/geo-lookups. Simplified for the web.
    • City list: Natural Earth populated places (public domain); city-centre points from Wikidata (CC0). Town and village names for search: Wikidata (CC0).
    • Base map: OpenFreeMap Positron style (OpenStreetMap, ODbL). MapLibre GL JS 4.7.1 (BSD 3-Clause), topojson-client 3.1.0 (ISC).
